# Product Overview

## What Is UXUY

UXUY is a stablecoin-native onchain trading platform **incubated by Binance Labs and backed by YZi Labs**. Using stablecoins alone, users can trade crypto assets, perpetual contracts, equities and real-world assets (RWA) within a single account and a single interface, without manually switching between multiple networks, wallets, bridges and trading protocols.

UXUY brings smart contract accounts, GasLess, cross-chain routing and onchain settlement together into one unified experience, realizing the vision of **"Buy Everything with U"**. Chain selection, gas management and cross-chain routing are handled automatically by the protocol in the background, while every transaction record remains publicly verifiable onchain.

Traders can manage assets and access different types of trading markets through the unified interface in the UXUY App or on the web.

## What Makes UXUY Different

- **Stablecoin-native**: Stablecoins serve as the unified unit of account and settlement medium, so users do not need to prepare native tokens for each chain or manage multiple wallets.
- **One account, multiple markets**: Crypto assets, perpetual contracts, equities and RWA are all handled within the same account and interface, with no need to migrate assets between platforms.
- **Automatic cross-chain routing**: The system automatically evaluates liquidity, price, slippage and network costs to find an executable path for each trade, so users do not need to select chains or bridge assets manually.
- **Onchain verifiable**: Settlement records are published onchain, allowing users to independently verify transactions and fund flows through block explorers.

## What You Can Do on UXUY

- Trade crypto assets across supported blockchain networks
- Trade perpetual contracts with leverage
- Access onchain equity and real-world asset (RWA) markets
- Deposit, transfer and withdraw supported assets
- Complete trades through cross-chain routing without manually switching networks

:::note
The availability of specific products and markets may vary by region and platform version.
:::

## Core Features

### One Account, Multiple Markets

Through a single UXUY account and a unified interface, users can access crypto assets, perpetual contracts, equities, RWA and other markets.

### GasLess Experience

For eligible transactions, UXUY can abstract away the gas requirements of blockchain networks, allowing users to complete onchain interactions without separately holding native gas tokens on every supported network.

Eligibility conditions, minimum transaction amounts and supported networks may vary. Final fees are shown on the page before a user confirms a transaction. See [GasLess Rules](./account-and-assets/gasless-rules.md) for details.

### Cross-Chain Asset Routing

UXUY identifies a user's available balances and executable paths across supported networks. After a user submits a transaction, the system evaluates available liquidity, price, slippage, network costs and routing status before executing the trade.

### Smart Contract Accounts

UXUY is built on smart contract account infrastructure, simplifying wallet creation and onchain interaction while preserving transparent, verifiable blockchain settlement records.

### Onchain Settlement

Trades are executed or settled through supported blockchain networks or protocols. Where applicable, users can look up and verify the corresponding transaction records via the relevant block explorer.
