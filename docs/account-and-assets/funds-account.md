# Funds Account

The Funds Account is the main entry point for users to view and manage assets supported by UXUY.

Through the Funds Account, users can:

- View balances of different supported assets
- Deposit assets from external wallets or trading platforms
- Withdraw assets to external addresses
- Transfer assets between available UXUY product accounts
- Where available, use supported fiat or third-party on-ramp channels

For detailed deposit and withdrawal steps, see [Getting Started](../getting-started.md#deposits-and-withdrawals).
