# Trading

## Trading Overview

UXUY provides access to a range of onchain markets through a single unified interface.

## Crypto Asset Trading

Users can trade supported crypto assets using the available stablecoin balance in their account. UXUY may route across different supported networks and liquidity sources to find an executable trading path.

Before confirming a trade, pay particular attention to:

- Estimated execution price
- Price impact and slippage
- Trading fees and network fees
- Minimum amount received
- Selected execution route and network

## Perpetual Contracts

Users can trade perpetual contracts using margin and leverage. Perpetual contracts have no fixed expiry date, but holding a position may incur funding costs. When account margin is insufficient, positions may be liquidated.

See [Perpetual Contracts](./perpetuals/overview.md) for the full rules.

## Equities and RWA

UXUY provides access to onchain markets for supported equity-linked assets and real-world assets (RWA). Trading hours, liquidity, price formation mechanisms, settlement structures and regional availability may differ across products.

:::note
During the non-trading hours of the primary market, liquidity may be lower and bid-ask spreads or price impact may increase.
:::

## Copy Trading

Copy trading allows users to follow eligible traders and automatically replicate their selected trading activity according to the copy parameters the user sets.

The availability of copy trading and its execution infrastructure are subject to the product version currently shown in the UXUY App.
