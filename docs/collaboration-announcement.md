# UXUY Wallet Collaboration Announcement

UXUY Wallet works with projects across its wallet and DApp ecosystem, offering project displays, livestream interaction, and in-person discussions to help users learn about ecosystem projects.

A project display or participation in an event does not, by itself, mean that UXUY Wallet has invested in a project, acts as its guarantor, or promises any returns. Each collaboration is limited to the specific activities confirmed by both parties.

## Collaboration Options

| Option | Overview |
| --- | --- |
| Featured DApp Listings | Project teams may apply for a listing in the wallet's featured DApp section, subject to submitting the required materials and meeting the applicable review and collaboration requirements. |
| Banner Displays | Projects that meet the project review and other applicable collaboration requirements may apply for banner displays. Banner displays and featured DApp listings require separate applications. |
| Livestream Collaboration | Project teams may host their own livestreams and work with UXUY Wallet on audience interaction and user engagement. |
| In-Person AMA Sessions | Eligible projects may apply to hold in-person discussions for their users. The parties agree on the timing, location, and specific arrangements. |

Application requirements, display periods, and implementation arrangements vary by option. Project teams may contact their UXUY Wallet collaboration representative for details. Submitting an application does not mean that collaboration eligibility or a display placement has been granted. Display positions and rankings are arranged by the operations team and may change during the display period. UXUY Wallet does not promise a fixed ranking, specific traffic levels or user growth, or particular trading results.

Where a livestream collaboration includes trial credits provided by UXUY Wallet, UXUY Wallet is responsible for distributing those credits within the scope of its agreed obligations. Participants should consult the applicable event rules for amounts, distribution timing, and restrictions on use.

## Application and Review

Project teams may contact their UXUY Wallet collaboration representative to identify the resources they wish to apply for and provide project information, DApp links, deployed smart contract details, and other materials required for verification. Where an application is subject to liquidity pool requirements, the project team must identify the relevant blockchain and pool address and provide supporting information to verify the pool's funds.

Applications for featured DApp listings must include an audit report covering the contracts currently deployed and meet the applicable audit and product verification requirements. UXUY Wallet will check core product workflows and relevant mechanisms for withdrawing funds. Whether an audit report is required for other collaboration options is determined on a project-by-project basis. These reviews and checks inform collaboration eligibility; they do not guarantee a project's continued security, legality, regulatory compliance, or the ability to withdraw funds.

Once an application is approved, the parties confirm the specific collaboration activities and implementation arrangements, proceed in line with the implementation schedule, and review the results and discuss next steps as appropriate to the selected option.

## Scope and Responsibilities

UXUY Wallet provides display services, user engagement activities, and event support within the scope confirmed by both parties. Each project team is responsible under applicable law for the project information, promotional content, products, smart contracts, and related services it provides. A project team's views, statements, and actions do not represent UXUY Wallet's official position.

Project reviews, audit report checks, project displays, and participation by relevant personnel in livestreams or AMA sessions do not guarantee a project's security, regulatory compliance, asset value, the ability to withdraw funds, or investment returns, nor do they constitute investment recommendations. UXUY Wallet's status as an issuer, operator, or guarantor of a project must not be inferred solely from such collaboration. The parties' relationships and responsibilities must be assessed in light of the actual arrangements.

Funds in the project liquidity pools referred to in this overview are provided and controlled by the project teams or users themselves. UXUY Wallet does not control those pool funds. This statement applies only to the liquidity pools involved in the collaborations described here.

These statements do not release UXUY Wallet from its expressly assumed obligations, including those relating to display services and the distribution of trial credits, or exclude or limit any liability of any party that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

## Participation Considerations

Before accessing or participating in a third-party project, users should understand its service provider, applicable rules, smart contract permissions, fees, and conditions for withdrawing funds. Digital assets and related projects may involve risks including price volatility, smart contract vulnerabilities, insufficient liquidity, changes in project operations, and loss of funds. Project displays and event support do not replace a user's own assessment of a project.

Being able to access this page does not mean that the related services or events are available or open to participation in your location. Eligibility is subject to applicable laws and the relevant participation requirements.
